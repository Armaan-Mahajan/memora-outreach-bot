# Memora — brand voice & visual identity

Pulled directly from the live landing page copy and design system as of 2026-08-28, so generated content can match what actually exists rather than inventing a new voice. This is the *site's* voice; outreach-bot-brief.md argues social content should shift a notch more peer-to-peer than this.

## Actual copy on the site today

- Hero headline (typing effect): "**Master** Memory / School / Studying"
- Hero subhead: "Unleash your learning potential with AI-powered study tools designed to help you remember more and learn faster."
- Features section header: "Everything you need to master any subject" / "Memora combines AI with proven learning science to build lasting knowledge."
- Memora Agent callout: "Transform your entire syllabus into a comprehensive interactive course — automatically. Available only on Memora."
- Closing CTA: "Ready to master your subjects?" / "Join students who are learning smarter with Memora's AI-powered study platform."
- Primary buttons: "Get started free" / "Try Agent" / "Go to Dashboard"
- Status badge on the hero: "Public release soon!" — the site openly presents itself as pre-launch, not as an established product.
- Footer tagline: "The AI-powered learning platform that helps you master any subject efficiently."

## Recurring vocabulary

"Master," "unleash," "smarter," "efficiently," "comprehensive," "adapt to what you know." Benefit-led, confident, verb-forward. Not jokey, not meme-heavy, not slang-driven — the current site voice reads more like competent SaaS marketing than like a Gen Z social account. That gap is intentional room for the outreach bot to occupy a different, more casual register without contradicting the brand — see outreach-bot-brief.md.

## Visual identity

- Primary/secondary gradient built from **violet through indigo to sky blue**; "glass" panels (translucent cards, soft blur, subtle 3D tilt on hover) are the dominant UI motif across the landing page.
- Social proof is shown via a small rotating "beta testers" strip, not large user-count claims — consistent with the product's actual pre-launch scale.
- Existing brand assets in the app repo's `public/` folder: `logo.svg` / `logo-inverted.svg` (wordmark), `og-image.png` (link-preview card), `social-media-logo.png` / `social-media-logo-inverted.png` (already sized for social use, unused so far).

## What to avoid

- Don't invent user counts, testimonials, or "join thousands of students" framing — the product says so itself ("Public release soon").
- Don't borrow generic edtech-marketing phrases not already in Memora's own copy without a reason; when leading with a feature, use Memora's own name for it (Mora, Memora Agent) rather than a generic description.
